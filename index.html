How Nexjob works

Four steps, in the order you actually do them.

  1. Compare before you apply

    Filter by pay on a ruler rather than a dropdown, and see every band against the same domain. A role paying under the market shows it immediately.

  2. Check what it takes to get in

    Each company has a page covering its interview loop stage by stage, what the rounds actually weight, and the internships and assessments it runs. See the routes.

  3. Apply, and watch the pipeline

    Every application you send sits on a track — applied, screening, interview, offer — so you always know which ones are still moving.

  4. Let the board come to you

    Fill in your profile once and the dashboard scores new roles against your skills, with the reasons shown rather than hidden.
