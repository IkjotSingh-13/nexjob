Keep track of where you stand.

An account is what turns a job board into a pipeline. Everything below is stored in this browser only — there is no server behind this project.

  • Applications land on a track you can follow from applied through to offer
  • Saved roles and alerts stay with you between visits
  • Your profile scores new roles against your own skills, with the reasons shown
  • Employers get a posting dashboard and an applicant pipeline
